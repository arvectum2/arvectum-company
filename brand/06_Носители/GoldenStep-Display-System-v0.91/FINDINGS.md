# Design findings and owner decision proposal v0.91

A controlled comparison distinguishes glyph authorship from a brand typography system.

**Font-only B1:** Inter vs v0.91 Golden Display differs by nine glyphs out of 2937. Body Text is exact unchanged Inter. At 390px mobile font-only difference occupies 0.00966% of full screenshot pixels. Open A/А is noticeable at 52–133px specimens, but ordinary carrier use remains visually similar to Inter.

**System B2:** Same Golden Display, same original Inter Text and content, with CSS adjustments limited to headlines: tighter tracking and a short proposed mint accent line. The full mobile pixel difference rises to 13.90735%; proposal page 1 rises to 7.63042%. Most of this comes from style and layout changes, not direct glyph differences, so it is not proof of improved aesthetics. Mobile layout remains within 390px and A4 page counts are unchanged.

**Recommendation, NOT an approved decision:** Keep Inter Text as the stable readable text face. Continue Golden Step Display as a headline candidate with open A/А, but evaluate the layout recipe independently from the font. Prioritize 32–68px headlines, mobile readability, congruence with the approved wordmark contours, appropriate tracking and accent restraint.

**Unresolved:** owner's optical approval for A/А, design-system color/spacing/hierarchy decisions, font metadata and SIL OFL release audit, human reading studies, PDF/X preflight and Windows qualification. No production site or client asset changed.
