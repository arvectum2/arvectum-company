# v0.112 authority and font source reconciliation

Owner instructed working font development to continue with a professional designer's judgement, following strict accepted W α and X/Х β angle requirements. Actual v0.111 test detected 9 legacy W-accent contours and unshifted mark anchors, a confirmed inconsistency not requiring a new owner design decision to correct in the reversible working prototype.

Earlier v0.108/109 forced-angle W alternatives and XSoft remain historical only; v0.110 alpha W is preserved.
