The reconstructed accent A family remains CLOSED; do not interpret this as an owner's final decision about signature behavior for accented letters.

W and all nine W accent variants keep the existing alpha construction, X/Х beta and historical K/К and Ж decisions untouched. Previous v0.107 extra cuts and v0.108/v0.109 W alternatives remain rejected.
