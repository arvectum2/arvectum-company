# Arvectum Golden Step v0.96 — phi round-family experiment

**Experimental** — not an owner-approved or production font. Based on v0.95 Display and v0.95 Text, both distinctly developed from Inter 4.1 under SIL OFL 1.1.

This iteration harmonizes O/О, o/о, C/С and c/с through a consistent local phi-derived Bézier-handle system. Stems and natural continuous curves remain; there are no polygonized bowls. The C/c apertures and digit 0 remain unchanged to protect small-size recognition.

- Eight modified glyph contours per optical master vs v0.95; 2929 unchanged.
- Twenty-eight modified glyph candidates per master cumulatively vs unmodified source Inter; 2909 remain exact Inter outlines.
- All 2937 glyphs and 2852 Unicode positions preserved; hmtx, GPOS/GSUB/GDEF and original bounds unchanged.
- Two OTF + two equivalent WOFF2, editable SVG before/after, five-page comparison PDF with full alphabets and Text at 12–24px, full-source script/QA provenance, SIL OFL license.
- Core alphabet CSV/JSON with 256 status rows: 28 candidates, 3 inspected inherited, 97 not reviewed per master.
- Separate real-carrier offline comparison with v0.94 in brand/06_Носители/GoldenStep-Family-Carrier-v0.96.

**Not yet established:** superior legibility, distinctive complete typeface, human optical approval, production usage or public redistribution clearance. Future character families (especially high-frequency Cyrillic lowercase) remain substantial work.
