# v0.86 source and decision boundary

- The owner rejected all three v0.84 З/з forms, then instructed to use Inter as the base and refine glyphs one by one.
- v0.85 provides the exact Inter 4.1 source glyphs; v0.86 modifies only uppercase and lowercase Cyrillic З/з, preserving their topology and creating small straight extrema.
- The font source remains derived from Inter v4.1, SIL OFL 1.1. The Inter reserved font name must not be used as the new font's primary family name; derivative family and PostScript names are Arvectum draft names. Upstream copyright and license are retained.
- This is a draft candidate, not a claimed improvement, not an accepted corporate typeface, and not a provenance claim of independent full-font authorship.
- Fonts v0.84 and earlier remain unchanged. Do not modify canonical wordmarks, website, installed fonts, contracts, Windows or Arvectum OS for this experiment.
- Mechanical vector and small-raster checks passed; optical owner review and font-release/legal gates remain open.
