# Arvectum Golden Step v0.94 — structural family extension

**Experimental, not production or final owner-approved.** 22 November 2026.

Continuation of the TWO separately designed Arvectum Display and Arvectum Text masters, with Inter 4.1 as upstream SIL OFL 1.1 construction source. v0.93 refined homologous E/Е, H/Н and M/М; v0.94 adds native-anatomy corrections to Latin K, Cyrillic К and Cyrillic И. Do not impose an identical K outline across scripts.

Latin K keeps its existing curved internal joint, Cyrillic К retains straight shoulders. Both get a small 51-unit waist raise from cap/φ⁷ in the appropriate different points. Cyrillic И receives x/y diagonal ratios near 1/φ with nearby source Bézier tangents preserved. See SPECIFICATION.md.

- **3 new glyphs/style** differ from v0.93; **2934 outlines unchanged**.
- **18 changed glyphs/style cumulatively vs unmodified Inter**, other 2919 remain Inter outlines. All Unicode positions (2852), OpenType layout and kerning, advance widths and overall glyph bounds retained.
- Two source CFF OTF, two WOFF2 with ALL 2937 glyph geometries confirmed identical, exact SVG before/after, 5-page vector PDF including Text 12/14/16/18/24px, evidence/build and QA reports.
- An auditable 256-row core inventory (128 basic characters × Display/Text) classifies each face: 18 modified candidates, 5 structurally inspected/inherited provisional (П, Т/T, Д, Л), and 105 not-yet-reviewed inherited.
- Existing П, T/Т and Д/Л geometry is kept with documented measurements; **optical approval still OPEN**.
- Source spec, build/proof/verify/audit scripts, license, checksums, zip review kit, historical v0.92/v0.93 unchanged.

This is not a finished independent typeface; it has not passed user artistic review, reader tests, full character-set QA, naming/rights review, Windows or PDF/X qualification. No live website, company wordmark, production font, customer file or Arvectum OS was modified.
