# Arvectum Golden Step v0.95 — Д/Л construction

Experimental, not production-ready. The company font is independently developed as Arvectum Display Medium and Arvectum Text Regular, both derived from Inter v4.1, not accepting the original Inter Text as final.

This version modifies only Cyrillic Д and Л (two glyphs/style). Both share the local phi^(-2) ratio of roof wedge to upper internal shelf, while retaining their native curved lower legs and connected cubic tangents. П and Т/T were inspected but provisionally left unchanged.

The two source OTF files, two WOFF2 fonts, editable SVG outlines, four-page A4 comparison and Text 12–32px specimen are saved with reproducible scripts and source hashes. Technical QA confirmed exact scope, bounds, widths, layout, unicode and shaping, not a human legibility improvement.

Cumulative vs untouched Inter: 20 changed candidates and 2917 unchanged outlines per master. Future development, optical acceptance, licensing/naming, Windows and print preflight remain open. No production changes.
