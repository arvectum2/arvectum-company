# Arvectum Golden Step v0.41 — curved and double-bowl family
2026-11-20 | BR-05 | Draft | Not approved for production

## Scope and geometry
Continue v0.40. Replace upstream B D U a g / Б В Ъ Ь а б Ы ы, 13 letter positions per optical style. B/В and a/а identical within each style. The upper and lower bowl division in B and the base bowl height in Б/Ь/Ъ use H/phi. Lower curvature in U uses H/phi²; hard-sign offset and separate Ы/ы stem spacing use t/phi. New curves use quadrant coefficient k=1/phi Display and k=0.5522847498 Text where applicable. Stem targets 200.59127/185.435, bar targets 190.77974/175, cap 1490, x-height 1056. The a/а bowl derives from preserved v0.38 q with descender removed; g shares this bowl with a new hooked descender. Single-storey a/а/g are draft design choices, not owner approval. D uses a half-ellipse joined to a vertical stem, б a curved rising terminal and horizontal upper flag, Ы/ы a soft-sign body plus separate right stem. Widths, flag and terminal shapes, joins and optical spacing are provisional. Existing masters and previous non-upstream outlines unchanged.
