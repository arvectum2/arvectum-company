# Arvectum Golden Step v0.93 — E/Е, H/Н, M/М

Experimental structural-capital research for the **two independently developing Arvectum masters** Display Medium and Text Regular. Inter v4.1 is their source, not the final adopted Text face.

Three matching Latin/Cyrillic pairs receive measured, conservative φ-driven geometry: a shorter and raised middle arm on E/Е, the related raised crossbar on H/Н, and a deeper but still curved central V on M/М. See SPECIFICATION.md and evidence/build-manifest.json for complete numeric construction.

- Exactly 6 new modified glyph outlines/style relative to v0.92, 2931 unchanged.
- All previous v0.92 right-opening Display A/А, closed customized Text A/А, З/з, В/в/B, Б/б remain preserved.
- Two OTF and two WOFF2 (2937/2937 full outline matches each), exact SVG outline comparison, 4-page vector PDF with both styles and basic alphabets, reproducible build/proof/verify scripts, source hashes and evidence.
- All 2852 Unicode positions and all original metrics/OpenType layout tables unchanged. HarfBuzz Russian/English shaping and 12–96px pixel comparison PASS.
- Human artistic acceptance, independent readability, commercial licensing/release and PDF printing preflight are OPEN. All artifacts are candidates, not production fonts.

The next independent family is K/К and И, followed by review of П, T/Т, Д/Л and other structural letters. Do not treat a ratio or machine PASS as a successful brand decision.
