# v0.93 provenance and authority

v0.93 derives from both actual v0.92 Golden Step masters (original Inter 4.1 source, SIL OFL 1.1). Neither face is the original or accepted Inter font; both are active Arvectum projects.

Only E/Е, H/Н, M/М are newly modified; prior A/А RIGHT Display opening and CLOSED customized Text are preserved. Unmodified earlier glyph contours and all metric/layout tables are identical to source v0.92. See exact hashes and changed list in evidence JSON.

The original wordmark, live company website, production templates, fonts, OS and earlier historical versions were not changed. No owner final artistic acceptance or public distribution permission is implied.
