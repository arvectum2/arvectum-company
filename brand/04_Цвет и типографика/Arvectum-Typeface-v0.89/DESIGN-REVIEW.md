# Optical checkpoint — complete alphabet / Golden Step v0.89

The PDF compares whole Cyrillic alphabets in Display and Text, with Ё/ё, complete Latin and digits, alongside focused Б/б and real-word specimens.

## Confirmed design lineage
- v0.85: exact Inter 4.1 outlines baseline.
- v0.87: only З/з modified through local φ-guided horizontal flats and bridge tuning.
- v0.88: only В/в/B modified, preserving inter-script identical capital В/B.
- v0.89: only Б/б modified, with existing upper/lower segments and small local flats.

Exactly **seven** glyphs per style now differ from the untouched Inter basis; 2930 remain original Inter. The drawings are intentionally restrained. Do not claim the entire alphabet is independently original or designed using golden ratios.

## Visual observation
At reading sizes, the changes remain subtle. No obvious letter clipping or line overflow appeared in the six-sheet visual review, but an optical improvement over Inter is not proven by geometry or file QA.

## Next evidence gate
Test new versions in actual company brand carriers: editable A4 letter and offer, an interface screen in 12–16px, and a printed small sample. Compare readability, rhythm, character differentiation and brand recognition against untouched Inter. Only introduce new custom glyphs where a specific need can be demonstrated, rather than creating revisions for version-count growth. Owner must approve artistic form and external release separately.
