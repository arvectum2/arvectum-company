# Arvectum Golden Step v0.44 — tabular lining digits
2026-11-20 | BR-05 | Draft | Not approved for production

## Scope and geometry
New 0–9 outlines for both styles, replacing 20 upstream digit positions. Historical v0.7 advances retained (UPM 2048, Display 1323, Text 1266). All figures use equal advance, centered ink and at least 50 design units of side bearing. Cap height 1490; rounded overshoot up to 20. Main stem/bar targets 200.59127/190.77974 Display, 185.435/175 Text. Newly constructed quadrant curves use k=1/phi or the Text optical coefficient .5522847498. The 0 has an isolated diagonal mark with golden slope, 1 has flag and foot, 7 has a golden diagonal, 3 adapts the v0.42 З family, 6/9 are rotational counterparts, bowl heights of 5/6/8 use H/phi. Exact digit widths, 2/4 diagonals and terminals, 0 mark and open 4 are provisional optical decisions, not recovered v0.7 original forms or owner approval. No claim that all curves/angles are governed by phi. 0 mark currently default, not an OpenType zero alternate.

## QA
20 SVG, sampled positive contour areas, strict intersection and counter nesting checks, expected counters and bounds. First 4 outline failed crossing check and was redesigned; 8 waist corrected to avoid a shared-point pinch. Two CFF OTF builds, each 180 codepoints; all ten digits changed per style and all 170 other positions identical in recorded outlines and hmtx. All advances including digits unchanged versus v0.43. FreeType samples at 12/16/24/40/72 px show nonempty digits, identical digit string advances and different raster masks for 0/O/О and 1/I. HarfBuzz verified the ten advances, no .notdef; comparison strings checked against cmap. Individually inspected before/after proof with amounts and dates; 3 versus З, 5/6 balance, narrow 1, isolated 0 mark and 4 terminal require final optical review. Raster difference is not a human readability study. The initial proof revealed missing U+007C vertical bar; replaced only the proof separator by an encoded em dash, recorded U+007C with U+00B7 as pending coverage. Two successive SVG/OTF builds byte-identical. No final kerning, GPOS/GSUB, install, print or release tests.

## Coverage and continuation
118 alphabet + 10 digit positions = 128/180 with non-upstream working outlines per style, including legacy adaptations. Remaining 52 positions per style: 49 punctuation/math/currency and 3 spaces. Next v0.45 punctuation, including at least U+00B7 and U+007C additions; then mathematical and currency symbols, whitespace, optical harmonization, metrics, kerning and OpenType. Tabular default remains; proportional figures and tnum/zero feature policy deferred. Historical v0.7 archive remains unavailable. Preserve all historical versions, approved logo masters, and OFL provenance in v0.36/sources.
