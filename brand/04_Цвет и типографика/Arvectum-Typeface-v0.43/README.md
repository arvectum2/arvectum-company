# Arvectum Golden Step v0.43 — complete working alphabet
2026-11-20 | BR-05 | Draft | Not approved for production

Scope: Ё Й М Ч г д ё й ф ч я (both styles) and r t т (Text only), 25 formerly upstream letter positions. Ё/ё and Й/й retain the compiled E/e Cyrillic and И/и base contours and advances, adding square dots or a curved breve. Mark height/gap/width use t/phi, .8t and base ink width/phi; breve curvature k=1/phi Display and .5522847498 Text. Cyrillic М copies the existing Latin M including advance. Ч/ч use curved arms and golden vertical division; г uses the t*phi³ width. д and я are affine adaptations of Д and Я; ф optically remaps Ф to lowercase heights. r/t/т are new Text candidates related to the Display wordmark-derived forms; they keep the golden stem/bar targets 185.435/175 but are not identical copies. All new width choices, marks, joins and adaptations are draft optical decisions, not approved historical metrics. Original masters remain unchanged.

QA: 25 SVG, sampled positive areas, strict segment crossing, nesting and expected counter counts, clearance for accents. 2 CFF OTFs, 180 encoded positions per style. 11 Display and 14 Text positions changed; 169/166 others identical by recorded outlines and hmtx. M/М and accented base contours compared after removing only redundant explicit closing lines from recording representations; advances unchanged. Initial exact record comparison failed because of the redundant closing segment after SVG serialization, resolved by geometric-equivalent normalization; raw comparisons outside scope remain exact. All 118 alphabet positions per style rasterize nonempty at 12/16/24/40/72 px and shape without .notdef in HarfBuzz. Full proof and before/after sheet visually inspected; successive SVG/OTF outputs byte-identical. No exhaustive readability or print validation; mark size, breve weight, triangular д, compact ф/я, Text r/t balance require optical review. Combining diacritics and normalization/shaping support not implemented; only precomposed letters tested.

Coverage: 118/118 alphabet positions in each style now have non-upstream working contours (includes earlier legacy outlines and adaptations, not necessarily original independent designs or approved shapes). No untouched Inter fallback letters remain. The remaining 62 encoded positions in each font are 10 digits, 49 punctuation/math/currency symbols and 3 spaces inherited from Inter; additional missing middle dot U+00B7 previously noted. Inter OFL provenance remains applicable; no claim of exclusive ownership or complete original typeface. Next v0.44: tabular digits, then punctuation/special symbols and whitespace metrics, missing symbol coverage, systematic optical review, spacing/kerning, OpenType/combining marks, print/system/web QA, owner approval. All previous versions retained. No installation, distribution or publication.

Reproduction: Python 3.14, numpy, fontTools 4.68.0; Pillow and hb-shape for proofs. Run alphabet.py, build.py, proof.py, compare.py. This iteration derives some outlines from the tracked compiled v0.42 OTFs. Artifacts: glyphs/, experimental/, glyph-provenance.csv, alphabet-geometry.json, coverage-audit.json, build-report.json, render-qa.json, comparison-qa.json, proof.png, alphabet-comparison.png, SHA256SUMS.txt.
