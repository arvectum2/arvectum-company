# Golden Step v0.55 — whitespace/vertical metrics (GS-008)
Two process-testable CFF OTF fonts from v0.54, with all 212 glyph outlines and hmtx unchanged. hhea +2080/-680; OS/2 typo +2080/-680, win ascent 2160, descent 760. This accommodates estimated composite accent extents +2065/-650, reducing clipping risk; 12pt nominal line box rises from 14.52pt to 16.17pt. This changes layout height and must be reviewed in real corporate templates. All digit widths remain tabular (1323 Display, 1266 Text). Ordinary U+0020 and NBSP U+00A0 both advance 512; narrow NBSP U+202F advances 316; HarfBuzz samples checked.

CoreText process registration (no OS font installation) succeeded for both OTFs and reports the new metrics and consistent figure advances. Narrow-line CTTypesetterSuggestLineBreak tests show 2 of 3 UTF-16 positions even after NBSP/NNBSP, indicating that a forced/emergency break may occur at narrow widths; therefore this cannot certify strict nonbreaking behavior in every engine. Unicode line-break policy is application-level and is not changed by the font glyph. Real office/browser wrapping remains part of final qualification. No owner typography approval, previous font versions or masters changed.
