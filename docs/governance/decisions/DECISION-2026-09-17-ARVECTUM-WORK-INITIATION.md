# DECISION-2026-09-17 — Arvectum Work separate product initiation

Status: `Approved / pending repository integration`
Decision date: `2026-09-17`
Decision class: `ROD-04 Portfolio/initiative + ROD-09 Company↔Product boundary`
Decision authority: `Owner of Arvectum Company`
Company repository: `arvectum2/arvectum-company`
Product repository: `arvectum2/arvectum-work`

## 1. Attributable Owner direction

The Owner explicitly directed on `2026-09-17`:

- start **Arvectum Work** as a separate product project;
- canonical GitHub repository: `arvectum2/arvectum-work`;
- GitVerse mirror: `arvectum/arvectum-work`;
- parent Company: `arvectum2/arvectum-company`;
- platform: `arvectum2/arvectum-os`;
- prioritize evidence of real solvent demand and the first paid end-to-end transaction before building a marketplace.

This is sufficient Company authority to establish a bounded product-discovery initiative and its ownership boundary.

## 2. Decision

Arvectum Company recognizes **Arvectum Work** as a separate bounded product-discovery initiative.

Current disposition:

`DISCOVER — evidence before material build`.

The product thesis is not treated as validated, profitable or approved for public marketplace launch.

## 3. Company-owned footprint

Company remains canonical for:

- portfolio identity and disposition;
- investment/capital allocation;
- risk appetite and material exceptions;
- Company-level priority changes;
- `Continue / Pivot / Stop` at the material investment gate;
- material Company↔Product↔Arvectum OS commitments.

The Company does not own the product's Job/Assignment/Execution/Result/Evidence/Acceptance/Settlement semantics.

## 4. Product-owned scope

`arvectum2/arvectum-work` is canonical for:

- discovery;
- Product Vision and hypotheses;
- customer/problem/market evidence;
- Work roadmap;
- business model and unit economics;
- product-specific semantics;
- Worker experiments;
- Product Contract proposals;
- architecture/implementation;
- product transaction evidence.

## 5. Arvectum OS boundary

This decision does not create or approve:

- an Arvectum Work Product Contract in Arvectum OS;
- a new Platform Capability;
- a mandatory Work→OS runtime dependency;
- marketplace semantics inside Arvectum OS.

Any future OS dependency/contract must follow the applicable Arvectum OS governance path independently.

## 6. Former Arvectum Exchange draft

Draft Company PR `#3 — AX-000: start Arvectum Exchange hypothesis discovery` is superseded by this separate-product decision.

It must not be merged as the product's canonical discovery artifact.

Reusable discovery content may be reconciled into Arvectum Work with explicit source/boundary discipline.

## 7. Discovery investment boundary

This decision authorizes product discovery and reversible repository/evidence work inside existing approved resources.

It does **not** by itself authorize:

- new incremental external spend;
- public marketplace launch;
- payment-provider commitments;
- custody/escrow or financial-intermediary activity;
- crypto settlement;
- material contracts or non-standard commercial exposure;
- production public-procurement integrations/submissions;
- claims of 44-FZ/223-FZ applicability or compliance without current transaction-specific verification;
- production deployment with material external effect;
- Arvectum OS contract/capability changes.

Any separately delegated normal-course commercial authority remains governed by its own source; this decision does not expand it.

## 8. Discovery success objective

The Company expects Work to seek, in this order:

1. buyer demand;
2. repeatable machine-executable Job family;
3. reliable Acceptance;
4. positive Worker/unit economics;
5. first real accepted paid end-to-end transaction;
6. repeat demand;
7. only then evidence that marketplace mechanisms are preferable to a managed service.

## 9. Owner gate

`AW-060 — Go / Pivot / Stop` is a material Company portfolio/investment gate.

A transition to `AW-100 — Marketplace MVP`, material new budget, public launch or materially broader external commitments requires the applicable explicit Owner/Company decision.

## 10. Priority boundary

This decision creates a bounded Owner-directed discovery initiative. It does not silently re-rank or cancel existing Company obligations/portfolio priorities.

Any material Company-level priority/capital trade-off must be decided using the current Company governance model.

## 11. Result

`APPROVED — Arvectum Work separate product discovery initiation, subject to repository integration and existing hard boundaries`.
