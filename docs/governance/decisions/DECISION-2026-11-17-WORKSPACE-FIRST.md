# DECISION-2026-11-17 — Workspace First

Status: Owner direction approved; repository publication pending merger.
Decision date: 2026-11-17
Decision authority: Owner of Arvectum Company
Scope: Company operating priorities and internal Workspace pilot; not an OS constitutional or contract amendment.

## Attributable Owner direction

The Owner selected option A: "давай пойдем по варианту А с собственной легкой CRM, которую можно доработать в будущем". Following the proposed Workspace First principle, the Owner explicitly directed: "да, давай зафиксируем этот принцип **Workspace First. теперь обнови дорожную (дорожные) карту (карты) и будем делать. потому что пока у меня так и не было еще успешного удобного опыта использования того, что мы разработали в рамках Arvectum OS**".

Owner-reported lack of successful convenient use is real qualitative negative evidence, not an invented metric or technical regression diagnosis. Prior scoped technical PASS remains historical evidence.

## Approved direction

Workspace First: Arvectum Workspace is the single default executive entry point for business-relevant information, linked context, decisions and work. New Arvectum products/processes should plan proportionate, explicitly contracted exchange of material management information; existing products integrate incrementally when useful, not by forced migration. Authoritative sources and product-local business semantics remain where they belong. No new central authority, blanket data-copy requirement, or mandatory shared runtime dependency.

Choose a lightweight first-party CRM within existing Productive Workspace, not a separate CRM repository, Twenty installation or deep fork. Information Hub and owner usability take precedence over feature breadth. Company owns requirements, operating decisions, classification, prioritization and acceptance. OS owns domain-neutral capabilities and platform governance; Workspace application code remains product-owned, logically separated in its existing OS repository pending evidence for extraction.

## Delivery priority and limits

Use the current Workspace, preserve existing data and Product Contract boundaries, and restore supported access before build expansion. Start with a bounded real internal workflow, preferably an existing Company project and permitted corporate sources. No cross-client or personal data ingestion without applicable rights and isolation. External spend baseline: zero incremental spend proposed; no purchase authorized. Existing client commitments, continuity and revenue work retain precedence. Tender mobile console is a product-specific surface, not a competing Company-wide source of truth; integrate material decisions via declared interfaces without forced migration.

The roadmap is a plan, not a grant of new AM-4 authority, new platform reliance, permission to deploy, access sensitive data, send communications, approve obligations, or incur external effects. Any new Product Contract/RFC/ADR, scope, spending or high-risk action follows applicable review/approval. No synthetic P10.07/P10.10 or Business PASS.

## Acceptance and review

Owner uses the real application for ordinary work, not a demo or a scripted approval. Record actual friction and time/coverage baseline, fix and re-test. Review after first usable slice and after five working days of real use (proposed pilot target). Aim for an accurate linked status brief within five minutes, no material recorded commitment without responsible party/next action, source-linked material facts and fewer manual context transfers. Measure rather than assert 30 minutes/day saved. No successful-owner-use claim without actual Owner feedback. If not useful, fix or contain the pilot before expanding.

Implementation planning: Company ROADMAP / STRATEGIC-ROADMAP and OS Workspace First delivery lane. Exact delivery details below those sources remain proposed until admitted under their applicable boundaries.
