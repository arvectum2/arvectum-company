## 2. Approved strategic framing

Arvectum is a machine-work company with two primary commercial delivery models:

1. **Buy the work — Arvectum Work.** The customer buys accepted machine-work outcomes.
2. **Own the capability — Arvectum AI Company.** The customer receives a governed customer-specific AI-native operating capability built on Arvectum OS.

Approved short thesis:

> **Arvectum turns business functions into governed machine work. A customer can buy the completed work through Arvectum Work or own the capability through an Arvectum AI Company implementation on Arvectum OS.**

## 3. Approved product-system interpretation

The Company portfolio is to be managed as one economic system, not as six independent Company strategies.

- **Arvectum Work** — work-as-a-service first; multi-executor marketplace only after evidence.
- **Arvectum AI Company** — high-value owned-capability implementation offer.
- **Arvectum OS** — domain-neutral organizational/governed-execution substrate, not a Company-specific marketplace.
- **Tender Agent** — anchor procurement capability plus standalone revenue/pilot path.
- **Creative Test Agent** — marketing/creative capability plus standalone product path where demand exists.
- **Discount Parser / Doors Parser** — product-specific assets and evidence for reusable data-acquisition/normalization patterns; not proof of a universal parser platform.
- **Data Platform** — no material build until named consumers/common contracts/economics justify it.
- **Proxy Launcher** — supporting infrastructure/ecosystem utility, not a core growth engine absent new evidence.

## 4. Strategic flywheel

Approved direction:

`paid Work demand → real process/economics evidence → reusable capability → owned-capability opportunity when economically justified`

and, where useful:

`owned AI organization → burst/overflow/missing capability → Arvectum Work`.

This is a commercial/strategic relationship, not an automatic hard runtime dependency.

## 5. Capital-allocation principle

Default strategic order:

1. obligations, continuity, cash and material risk;
2. real external revenue/customer acceptance evidence;
3. proof of Arvectum Work demand and flagship AI Company customer value;
4. capability reuse that measurably lowers cost or enables revenue;
5. Arvectum OS work required by admitted product/customer needs;
6. speculative platform/infrastructure/new-product work.

Existing exact contractual/client obligations retain their own priority.

## 6. Evidence discipline

Strategy approval does not prove:
- demand;
- profitability;
- CAC/LTV;
- legal/payment readiness;
- Work marketplace liquidity;
- AI Company deployment economics;
- module reuse economics;
- customer readiness.

Technical PASS remains insufficient for Business PASS.
