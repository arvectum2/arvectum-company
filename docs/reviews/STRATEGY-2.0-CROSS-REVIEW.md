# Arvectum Company Strategy 2.0 — Cross-Review

Status: `Review complete / ready for Owner decision`
Date: `2026-09-17`
Artifact under review: `docs/business/STRATEGY-2.0-PROPOSAL.md`
Maximum iterations: `7`

## Review method

The proposal was challenged through seven lenses. The purpose was not to maximize conceptual completeness, but to test whether the strategy makes Arvectum more economically coherent while preserving current Company/Product/OS boundaries.

## Iteration 1 — Business coherence

Question: does the strategy reduce portfolio fragmentation or merely rename it?

Result: `PASS`.

The proposal reduces the Company from multiple independent product stories to two economic engines: Work-as-a-Service and owned AI capability. Existing products become capability/revenue paths rather than six separate Company strategies.

Key retained correction: repository count is not strategic product count.

## Iteration 2 — Customer economics and cannibalization

Question: can Work and AI Company coexist without fighting for the same customer revenue?

Result: `PASS with explicit routing principle`.

The strategy should optimize for the economically appropriate delivery model, not protect one revenue stream. Work is preferable where integration burden and volume do not justify ownership. Owned capability is preferable when recurring spend/process stability crosses the automation threshold.

Important principle retained in proposal: Arvectum must not keep a customer on Work when an owned solution is clearly economically better solely to preserve recurring Work revenue.

## Iteration 3 — Product and authority boundaries

Question: does the strategy accidentally turn Arvectum OS into a Company-specific marketplace platform or create hidden cross-repository commitments?

Result: `PASS`.

The proposal preserves:

- Company ownership of strategy/capital/portfolio;
- Work ownership of machine-work product semantics;
- OS ownership of domain-neutral organizational contracts;
- product ownership of tender/marketing/data semantics.

The Work↔AI Company relationship is strategic and commercial, not an automatic runtime dependency.

## Iteration 4 — Capability portfolio discipline

Question: does Strategy 2.0 prematurely declare all existing products reusable modules?

Result: `PASS`.

Tender Agent is the strongest current vertical capability candidate. Creative Test Agent is a marketing capability candidate. Discount Parser and Doors Parser are evidence for reusable data patterns, not proof of a universal parser platform. Data Platform remains blocked from material build until named reuse consumers and common contracts exist.

This avoids architecture-first generalization.

## Iteration 5 — Go-to-market and demand learning

Question: does the strategy create a credible way to learn what customers will actually pay for?

Result: `PASS with evidence dependency`.

Arvectum Work is strategically valuable even before marketplace liquidity because it can act as a paid demand-sensing layer. Real Work streams produce pricing, acceptance, exception, volume and repeat-demand evidence that can identify future owned-capability opportunities.

However, Strategy 2.0 does not itself prove acquisition channels, ICP or sales efficiency. Those remain roadmap/customer-discovery evidence requirements.

## Iteration 6 — Owner workload and operating model

Question: will the strategy reduce or increase Owner bottleneck?

Result: `PASS conditionally`.

The two-engine model is viable only if normal execution is delegated to governed Positions/runtimes and the Owner remains focused on capital, risk, material commitments and strategy.

Therefore future roadmap gates must measure Owner intervention per Work stream, per deployment and per reusable capability. Revenue that requires the Owner to manually rescue routine execution is not strategic scale evidence.

Arvectum Company itself remains the first reference organization and proving ground, but internal dogfooding must not substitute for external customer evidence.

## Iteration 7 — Risk, sovereignty and strategic focus

Question: does the combined model create unbounded legal/payment/data/infrastructure exposure?

Result: `PASS for proposed strategy / not a launch authorization`.

The strategy correctly leaves payment custody, marketplace settlement, procurement paths, public launch, external commitments and production authority to later product/legal gates. Russia-first sovereignty remains a differentiator hypothesis, not a claim of compliance.

Proxy Launcher is correctly kept as supporting infrastructure/ecosystem rather than forced into the main monetization race.

## Final synthesis

Cross-review conclusion: `PASS — ready for Owner strategic decision`.

The strongest Strategy 2.0 formulation is:

> **Arvectum turns business functions into governed machine work. A customer can buy the completed work through Arvectum Work or own the capability through an Arvectum AI Company implementation on Arvectum OS. Reusable capabilities are learned from real paid work, customer deployments and Arvectum's own operations.**

## Material evidence gaps after strategic approval

Approval of the framing would not prove the business model. The next strategic roadmap must explicitly close these evidence gaps:

1. first repeatable external revenue and customer acceptance;
2. paid Arvectum Work demand with positive contribution margin;
3. a real flagship AI Company design partner / deployment economics;
4. measurable Work→owned crossover and/or owned→Work overflow case;
5. reuse economics for at least one vertical capability;
6. acquisition effort/CAC evidence for both commercial engines;
7. Owner-intervention reduction as volume grows;
8. legal/payment/data readiness for each actual transaction model.

## Recommended Owner decision boundary

If the Owner approves Strategy 2.0, promotion should change Company strategic framing and roadmap interpretation, but should **not** by itself:

- allocate new external spend;
- authorize public marketplace launch;
- change any Arvectum OS Product Contract;
- merge product repositories;
- create a universal parser/data platform;
- authorize payment custody or settlement commitments;
- grant new external execution/signing/procurement authority.

Those remain separate governed decisions.
