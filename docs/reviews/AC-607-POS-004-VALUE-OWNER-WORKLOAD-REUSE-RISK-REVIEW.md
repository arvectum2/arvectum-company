## 9. Iteration 7 — Strategic fit and residual risk

Result: `PASS for continuation / do not expand authority from this review`.

Strategy 2.0 increases the value of this Position because Company execution should move away from Owner-operated repository work while Owner attention shifts toward customer demand, revenue, capital and material risk.

The correct next posture is not more AI-workforce architecture. It is:
- keep the existing bounded POS-004 execution contour;
- collect lightweight cost/workload evidence during normal use;
- move Company attention to external commercial evidence.

Residual risks:
- same account/technical credentials can obscure whether an action was human or automation unless durable execution evidence is preserved;
- fully-loaded economics remain unmeasured;
- review/exception load can still grow with throughput;
- technical success can consume capacity without producing revenue;
- AM-4 must remain fail-closed at review, authority, external-effect and material-risk boundaries.
