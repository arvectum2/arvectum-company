# Arvectum Company — Strategic Roadmap

Status: `Active`
Version: `1.0.0`
Date: `2026-09-18`
Owner: `ООО «Арвектум»`
Strategy: `docs/business/COMPANY-STRATEGY-v2.0.0.md`

## 1. Purpose

This roadmap answers one Company-level question:

> **How do today's Arvectum assets become a viable, profitable and owner-manageable machine-work company?**

It is a strategic roadmap, not a replacement for product roadmaps or the operational Company roadmap.

Sequence is evidence-gated, not date-gated.

## 2. North-star sequence

```text
foundation / obligations
        ↓
first external paid acceptance
        ↓
prove Arvectum Work paid demand
        +
prove AI Company customer value
        ↓
validate reusable capabilities
        ↓
prove Work ↔ owned-capability routing
        ↓
standardize and scale both engines
        ↓
marketplace / standalone-platform expansion only if evidenced
```

## SR-0 — Strategic alignment and operating readiness

**Current / integration phase**

Objective: make the Company operate under one coherent product/economic model without disrupting current obligations.

Required:
- Strategy 2.0 canonical;
- Arvectum Work preserved as Discovery until its own gates pass;
- current client/product obligations remain explicit;
- AC-607 Owner-workload/value review completed;
- product/OS boundaries preserved;
- no speculative Data Platform or marketplace build.

Gate:
- strategy and portfolio roles are canonical;
- no hidden cross-repository dependency;
- current operating responsibilities have accountable Positions;
- Owner intervention baseline exists.

## SR-1 — First external revenue and acceptance

Objective: prioritize real paid/customer evidence over additional architecture.

Primary paths:
- record the Discount Parser client loss and reassess any further investment; do not count it as an active acceptance/revenue path;
- obtain real restricted paid-pilot evidence for Tender Agent where commercially available;
- move Arvectum Work through focused demand validation toward its first paid accepted batch/stream;
- use Creative Test Agent only on a real customer/design-partner trigger;
- maintain Proxy Launcher quality without making it a growth program.

Gate:
- at least one real external paid accepted outcome is attributable to Arvectum;
- revenue, direct cost, rework and Owner intervention are measured;
- no internal demo is counted as customer acceptance.

## SR-2 — Prove both commercial engines

Objective: establish that Arvectum has two credible ways to monetize machine work.

### Work proof
Required:
- repeat paid work stream or repeated paid batches;
- frozen acceptance model;
- positive or credibly improvable contribution margin;
- customer residual handling measured;
- repeat/continuation signal;
- Owner not acting as hidden routine operator.

### AI Company proof
Required:
- real external design partner/customer;
- paid discovery, pilot or implementation commitment;
- explicit business function and measurable customer outcome;
- bounded deployment scope and authority model;
- delivery/support economics measured.

Gate:
- both engines have external paying-customer evidence;
- one cannot be supported solely by internal dogfooding.

## SR-3 — Capability reuse and economic routing

Objective: prove that the portfolio creates leverage rather than maintenance burden.

Required:
- at least one vertical capability reused across two meaningful contexts (for example standalone + Work, or standalone + AI Company);
- reuse lowers cost/time or improves quality measurably;
- Work owned-automation crossover model is operational;
- at least one real Work→owned or owned→Work handoff/overflow case where economically appropriate;
- any OS dependency is admitted through its own governance, not by strategy assumption.

Gate:
- one evidenced reusable capability;
- one evidenced cross-engine routing case;
- no universal module created solely for architecture elegance.

## SR-4 — Standardize repeatable delivery

Objective: turn proofs into repeatable commercial operations.

Work:
- standard work-stream contracts/SKUs where evidence supports them;
- repeatable intake, execution, acceptance and billing;
- quality/exception dashboards;
- governed executor replacement and fallback.

AI Company:
- standard discovery/diagnosis method;
- implementation playbook;
- reusable capability catalog with admission criteria;
- customer handover/support model;
- recurring runtime/support packaging where proven.

Company:
- acquisition channel evidence;
- margin by engine;
- Owner-intervention targets and escalation-only normal state;
- operating Positions rather than Owner-owned routine queues.

Gate:
- repeat customers/deployments;
- delivery no longer depends on bespoke Owner rescue;
- support burden and contribution economics remain acceptable.

## SR-5 — Scale the two engines

Objective: grow revenue without multiplying independent product motions.

Priorities:
- deepen the best Work categories instead of adding many shallow categories;
- replicate the best AI Company deployment patterns;
- use Work demand to choose new capability investment;
- use deployment reuse to lower marginal cost;
- maintain sovereignty/security/legal controls proportional to external effect.

Gate:
- repeatable acquisition and delivery;
- positive unit economics by main engine;
- capability reuse is a net economic benefit;
- Owner is not the routine throughput bottleneck.

## SR-6 — Marketplace / network layer, only if justified

Status: `NOT ADMITTED by Strategy 2.0 alone`.

Consider external Workers / routing / marketplace mechanics only when managed Work has evidence that multiple executors materially improve:
- capacity;
- latency;
- price/quality;
- resilience;
- geographic/data constraints;
- or liquidity.

Before admission require:
- real buyer demand;
- multiple credible suppliers/Workers;
- settlement/legal model;
- acceptance/dispute model;
- fraud/quality controls;
- evidence that marketplace economics beat simpler managed delivery.

Public marketplace, payment custody and regulated settlement remain separate Owner/legal gates.

## SR-7 — Optional platform/ecosystem expansion

Status: `Evidence-triggered`.

Possible later motions:
- separate Arvectum OS platform sales only if customers demonstrate independent demand/capability to consume it;
- Proxy Launcher enterprise/commercial packaging only if demand appears;
- new platform/shared-data components only after multiple named consumers.

No expansion is required for Company success.

## 4. Strategic metrics

Company-level review should track:
- external revenue and cash contribution;
- repeat revenue;
- Work contribution margin;
- AI Company deployment margin;
- acquisition effort/CAC where measurable;
- customer acceptance/repeat signal;
- capability reuse savings/uplift;
- Owner intervention per paid stream/deployment;
- support burden;
- material incidents/legal/data exceptions.

Repository activity, test count and agent count are not north-star metrics.

## 5. Capital discipline

Default allocation:
`obligations/risk → external paid evidence → dual-engine proof → reuse → required OS → speculative expansion`.

A new product, department, universal module, marketplace layer or shared platform component requires an evidence-based business case and applicable authority.

## 6. Stop / Pivot rules

Pivot or stop a path when:
- external buyers will not pay for the outcome;
- modern employee+AI or off-the-shelf automation dominates economically;
- delivery margin remains structurally negative;
- Owner/manual rescue is intrinsic rather than transitional;
- legal/data/sovereignty constraints destroy the target economics;
- reuse creates more maintenance cost than value;
- marketplace mechanisms do not improve the managed model.

## 7. Immediate Company focus

The immediate strategic focus is:

1. complete the AC-607 Owner decision gate without making M6 economic proof a blocker for commercial work;
2. obtain external paid/acceptance evidence from live opportunities; Discount Parser no longer supplies an active customer path;
3. continue Arvectum Work AW-020→AW-050 evidence path without premature marketplace build;
4. find the first real AI Company design partner/customer;
5. measure capability reuse only after real consumers appear.

The next strategic review should happen when SR-1 evidence materially changes or when AW-060 reaches its Company Go/Pivot/Stop gate, whichever comes first.
