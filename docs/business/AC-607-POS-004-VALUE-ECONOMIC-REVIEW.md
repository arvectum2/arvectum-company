## 1. Decision question

Has the first AI-held Position produced enough real value to continue, and is the evidence sufficient to close M6 as an economic and operational proof?

## 2. Evidence conclusion

The answer separates two questions.

### Continue the Position?

**Yes — evidence supports continued bounded operation.**

Operational evidence shows:
- substantive Product execution;
- zero Owner execution interventions after enqueue in the first real AC-605 Product run;
- deterministic fail-closed behavior;
- durable evidence and review gates;
- executor replacement without authority transfer;
- later bounded roadmap execution that can proceed after one explicit Owner authorization rather than repeated intermediate instructions.

### Close M6 as an economic proof?

**Not yet.**

The Company still lacks:
- a historical or prospective comparable Owner-time baseline sufficient for monetary savings;
- attributable fully-loaded runtime/provider cost;
- representative rework/review cost;
- cross-product reuse economics.

No ROI number is defensible from current evidence.

## 3. Business value interpretation

POS-004 is valuable primarily because it reduces routine engineering coordination and allows the Owner to retain control at material gates rather than act as the step-by-step executor.

That value mechanism is now evidenced operationally.

It is strategically aligned with Strategy 2.0 because Owner attention should move toward:
- external customer evidence;
- Work demand;
- AI Company design partners;
- capital/risk/portfolio decisions.

Continuing to invest in AI-workforce architecture beyond the current bounded contour would have lower current business priority than proving external revenue.

## 4. Economic interpretation

Current evidence supports:

`bounded incremental cash exposure + real useful execution + reduced intermediate Owner actions`.

It does **not** support:

`proven fully-loaded positive ROI`.

Unknown cost is not zero. Owner time saved is not assigned an invented monetary value.

## 5. Reuse interpretation

The following are validated Company organizational assets:
- task admission contract;
- bounded authority envelope;
- fail-closed gates;
- durable execution evidence;
- explicit human/Owner review boundary;
- executor replacement;
- roadmap-driven execution pattern.

This is not enough to declare a universal software module, Arvectum OS capability or customer product.

## 6. Risk interpretation

The current contour is acceptable for continued bounded internal repository work because:
- authority remains explicit;
- external effects remain excluded without separate approval;
- automatic merge is conditional and reversible;
- review/counter gates fail closed;
- no automatic failover silently changes executor/provider;
- no material incident is evidenced in the reviewed contour.

The main remaining risk is organizational: engineering automation can optimize repository throughput while the Company still has insufficient external revenue. Strategy 2.0 therefore requires commercial evidence to dominate the next Company focus.

## 7. Proposed result

`AC-607 — Complete / CONDITIONAL PASS`.

Proposed M6 state:

`Current / operational value, fail-closed governance and continuity proven; economic quantification incomplete`.

Continue the current bounded POS-004 / AI-ENG-001 contour under its existing authority source. Do not broaden authority from this review.

Collect lightweight economics during ordinary runs rather than creating a separate engineering project:
- intervention count/class;
- coarse Owner time only where practical;
- provider/runtime cost where available;
- rework/rollback;
- accepted task outcome.

M6 should not block the Company from moving to external revenue evidence.

## 8. Owner approval requested

Owner approval should explicitly choose one of:

- **A — Approve recommended conditional result:** continue POS-004, AC-607 complete, keep M6 open for economic evidence;
- **B — Close M6 despite the economic evidence gap:** requires an explicit Owner decision that the current evidence is sufficient for the intended milestone meaning;
- **C — Narrow/stop the Position:** if operational value is judged insufficient relative to supervision/risk.

The review recommends **A**.
