## 1. Strategic thesis

Arvectum is building a Russian machine-work company with two primary commercial delivery models:

1. **Buy the work — Arvectum Work.** The customer transfers a bounded digital work stream and pays for accepted results, volume, quality and deadline.
2. **Own the capability — Arvectum AI Company.** The customer receives its own governed AI-native operating contour built on Arvectum OS with appropriate functional capabilities and human/AI/software executors.

Short form:

> **Arvectum turns business functions into governed machine work. Customers can either buy the completed work or own the capability that performs it.**

This proposal does not replace the Company Constitution. It refines the commercial strategy under its business-first, owner-control and sovereignty principles.

## 2. Why Strategy 2.0

The previous Company baseline correctly established «ИИ-компания под ключ» as the flagship commercial direction and Arvectum OS as the domain-neutral substrate. Since then, Arvectum Work has introduced a second credible route to monetization: sell recurring machine-executable outcomes before requiring the customer to buy or operate its own automation.

The portfolio should therefore stop being interpreted as six separate product bets. It should be managed as one system:

- two customer-facing economic engines;
- one reusable capability portfolio;
- one domain-neutral organizational substrate;
- a small supporting infrastructure/ecosystem layer.

## 4. The two commercial engines

### 4.1 Arvectum Work — work-as-a-service first

Arvectum Work should initially sell:

`volume + result + quality + deadline + price`

The customer should not need to operate prompts, models, retries or workers as part of normal use.

The initial model may be managed and supply-side concentrated in Arvectum itself. Marketplace or multi-executor routing is a later hypothesis, admitted only if it creates measurable capacity, price, quality or liquidity value.

Work should optimize for:

- fast time-to-value;
- low customer integration burden;
- clear acceptance criteria;
- measurable cost per accepted unit/batch;
- repeat purchase;
- positive contribution margin;
- low Owner intervention.

### 4.2 Arvectum AI Company — owned capability

The flagship high-value implementation offer remains:

> **«ИИ-компания под ключ»: governed organizational structure, workflows, authority, knowledge and AI/software execution adapted to the customer's business model on top of Arvectum OS.**

The customer buys an owned or customer-specific operating capability rather than recurring externally delivered work.

Likely value capture mechanisms remain:

- discovery/implementation fee;
- integration/customization fee;
- recurring runtime/support/operations;
- module or capability licensing/service uplift where justified.

Arvectum OS should normally be commercialized through this outcome-oriented offer rather than as a generic empty platform unless later evidence supports a separate platform product motion.

## 5. The Work ↔ Owned Capability flywheel

The two engines should feed each other.

### 5.1 Work → AI Company

Arvectum Work discovers which business functions customers actually pay to outsource as machine work.

For each recurring work stream Work accumulates evidence:

- real volume;
- real inputs/outputs;
- exception rate;
- acceptance rules;
- latency/SLA;
- execution and verification cost;
- customer residual handling;
- repeat demand.

When recurring Work spend, process stability and customer volume cross an economic threshold where owned automation becomes better, Work should surface an explicit handoff opportunity to Arvectum AI Company.

Conceptually:

`buy Work → prove recurring process → reach owned-automation crossover → deploy owned capability`

### 5.2 AI Company → Work

A customer operating on Arvectum OS may still need temporary capacity, burst processing or a capability not worth owning.

In such cases its organization may route bounded external work to Arvectum Work, subject to product, authority, data and legal boundaries.

Conceptually:

`owned organization → overflow/missing capability → Work → accepted external result`

This relationship is strategic, not an automatic hard runtime dependency.

## 6. Capability portfolio

Existing products should be treated as standalone products where commercially justified and as capability candidates where reuse evidence exists.

### 6.1 Procurement / Tender

Current anchor: Arvectum Tender Agent.

Strategic roles:

- standalone revenue/pilot product;
- procurement/tender capability for AI Company deployments;
- potential Worker/capability inside Work for bounded procurement-document operations;
- reference domain for high-consequence governed execution.

Do not broaden to unattended procurement submission or legal commitment without separate authority and product proof.

### 6.2 Marketing / Creative

Current anchor: Creative Test Agent.

Strategic roles:

- standalone marketing-analysis product;
- marketing/creative capability for customer AI organizations;
- potential Work supply for bounded creative-analysis and reporting jobs.

### 6.3 Data acquisition / normalization

Current evidence: Discount Parser, Doors Parser and related source-specific work.

Strategic role is not to declare a universal Parser product by fiat, but to identify reusable contracts only after multiple real consumers need the same patterns such as:

`collect → normalize → deduplicate → classify → preserve provenance → emit structured result`

Data Platform remains clarification-only until named consumers, shared contracts and economics justify material build.

### 6.4 Future capabilities

New capabilities should preferentially originate from one of three evidence sources:

1. repeated paid Work demand;
2. repeated AI Company implementation need;
3. real Arvectum Company internal workload with clear reuse value.

Repository count is not a reason to create a module.

## 7. Arvectum OS strategic role

Arvectum OS remains the domain-neutral Executable Organizational Model and governed execution substrate.

It owns universal platform semantics such as Identity, Organization, Authority, Records, Relationships, Provenance, Documents, Knowledge, Security, Persistence and Governed Execution where admitted by OS governance.

Arvectum OS must not absorb Company-specific marketplace, tender, marketing or parser semantics.

Commercially, OS should be primarily presented as the substrate that makes owned AI organizations governable, replaceable, auditable and sovereign.

A separate generic OS sales motion is deferred until evidence shows that customers want and can successfully consume the substrate independently.

## 8. Proxy Launcher strategic role

Arvectum Proxy Launcher should remain outside the main growth race unless new evidence changes its economics.

Current role:

- useful standalone infrastructure utility;
- Russian desktop/Linux credibility;
- distribution and brand trust;
- release/security/signing/sovereignty proving ground;
- potential supporting utility for later Arvectum deployments.

Default strategy: maintain quality and usefulness without forcing premature monetization or a hard dependency from core products.

## 9. Customer journey

The preferred commercial journey is no longer a single funnel.

### Entry A — customer wants an outcome now

`Painful digital work → Arvectum Work pilot → repeat paid stream → stay on Work OR handoff to owned capability`

### Entry B — customer already needs structural transformation

`Operational bottleneck → AI Company discovery → Arvectum OS + capabilities → recurring operation/support`

### Entry C — narrow standalone need

`Tender / Marketing / Parser / utility need → standalone product → possible expansion into Work or AI Company`

The Company should preserve all three entry paths while converging successful customers toward the model that creates the best joint economics rather than forcing every customer into one product.

## 10. Economic logic

### Work engine

Primary metrics:

- paid accepted units/batches;
- repeat purchase;
- revenue and GMV where applicable;
- execution cost;
- verification/rework cost;
- contribution margin;
- owner intervention;
- acquisition effort;
- time-to-result.

### AI Company engine

Primary metrics:

- implementation revenue;
- delivery cost and time;
- reusable capability ratio;
- recurring runtime/support revenue;
- support burden;
- customer ROI/value evidence;
- owner intervention;
- gross/contribution margin by deployment.

### Capability economics

A capability is strategically reusable only when reuse reduces marginal delivery cost, improves quality/speed, or creates independent revenue without creating disproportionate support/maintenance burden.

Shared-platform investment must remain distinguishable from product/customer-direct cost.

## 11. Strategic moat

Current moat hypotheses are cumulative rather than dependent on one feature:

- Russia-first legal, financial and infrastructure compatibility;
- RUB/SBP/digital-ruble readiness where legally and technically applicable;
- local/self-hosted execution and data sovereignty;
- organization-first authority/governance model through Arvectum OS;
- capability evidence from real Work and customer deployments;
- reusable execution history, acceptance models and provenance;
- Work-to-owned and owned-to-Work economic routing;
- replaceable models/runtimes rather than vendor-locked organizational meaning;
- Arvectum Company itself as a real dogfooding reference organization.

Each moat item remains a hypothesis until supported by market or operational evidence.

## 12. Portfolio optimization

Strategy 2.0 proposes the following conceptual roles, without silently changing current canonical portfolio dispositions:

| Portfolio node | Strategy 2.0 role |
|---|---|
| Arvectum Work | primary commercial engine: buy the work |
| Arvectum OS | platform substrate for governed owned capability |
| AI Company offer | primary high-value commercial engine: own the capability |
| Tender Agent | anchor vertical capability + standalone product |
| Creative Test Agent | marketing capability + standalone product candidate |
| Discount Parser | current standalone client product + data-pattern evidence |
| Doors Parser | contained source-specific evidence/reference |
| Data Platform | no material build until reuse demand is proven |
| Proxy Launcher | supporting infrastructure/ecosystem utility |

The strategic objective is to reduce independent product motions, not necessarily repository count.

## 13. Capital and execution discipline

Default capital-allocation order under Strategy 2.0:

1. obligations, continuity, cash and material risk;
2. evidence that creates real revenue or customer acceptance;
3. Work demand/paid-transaction discovery and flagship AI Company market proof;
4. capability reuse that measurably lowers delivery cost or enables revenue;
5. platform work required by admitted product/customer needs;
6. speculative infrastructure and new product creation.

Explicit Owner decisions may change this order.

No initiative gains budget, authority or production status merely by being shown in this strategy.

## 14. What Strategy 2.0 explicitly rejects

Arvectum should not optimize for:

- the largest possible catalog of agents;
- six independent go-to-market motions for six repositories;
- marketplace infrastructure before paid work demand;
- generic platform engineering without admitted consumers;
- premature universalization of product-specific code;
- forcing every customer to deploy Arvectum OS;
- forcing every Work customer to migrate to owned automation;
- hiding when owned automation is economically better than Work;
- keeping a customer on Work when a cheaper owned solution is clearly better solely to preserve recurring revenue;
- unlimited AI authority or hidden human completion represented as autonomy.

## 15. Company-level strategic success condition

Arvectum reaches a stronger business state when it can repeatedly do all of the following:

1. identify a costly digital business function;
2. sell and deliver that function as accepted machine work with positive economics;
3. recognize when the function should remain external Work versus become customer-owned capability;
4. deploy the owned version using Arvectum OS and reusable capabilities where economically justified;
5. reuse validated capabilities across customers without losing authority, data or sovereignty boundaries;
6. operate this system without the Owner becoming the routine execution bottleneck.

## 16. Strategy-to-roadmap implications

A separate Company strategic roadmap should be built after review of this proposal.

Its top-level sequence should prioritize evidence rather than repository completion:

`first real revenue/acceptance → prove Work demand and first paid streams → prove flagship AI Company customer value → validate capability reuse → establish Work↔owned handoff → scale repeatable commercial engines → admit marketplace/multi-executor layer only when economically justified`

Existing Company roadmap items and product roadmaps remain in force until explicitly reconciled and updated through their own governance paths.
