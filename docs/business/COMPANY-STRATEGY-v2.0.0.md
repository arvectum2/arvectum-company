## 1. Strategic identity

Arvectum builds governed machine work.

> **Arvectum turns business functions into governed machine work. Customers can either buy the completed work or own the capability that performs it.**

The Company has two primary commercial delivery models:

- **Buy the work — Arvectum Work**;
- **Own the capability — Arvectum AI Company**, built on Arvectum OS.

This strategy refines the older «ИИ-компания под ключ» flagship framing by adding a second, lower-friction commercial engine. It does not replace the Company Constitution or Arvectum OS governance.

## 3. Commercial engine A — Arvectum Work

Arvectum Work sells:

`volume + accepted result + quality + deadline + price`.

The customer should not need to operate prompts, models, retries or workers as the normal production process.

Current strategic sequence:

`managed machine work → repeatable paid streams → multi-executor routing only if useful → marketplace only if liquidity/economics justify it`.

Work is the Company's demand-sensing engine: paid streams reveal which business functions have real budgets, repeat demand, acceptance rules, exception rates and economically meaningful automation opportunities.

## 4. Commercial engine B — Arvectum AI Company

Arvectum AI Company is the high-value owned-capability offer:

> **«ИИ-компания под ключ»: governed organizational structure, Positions, authority, workflows, knowledge and AI/software execution adapted to the customer's business model on Arvectum OS.**

The customer buys a customer-specific operating capability, not a swarm of agents.

Likely value capture:
- paid discovery/diagnosis;
- implementation/integration;
- customization;
- recurring runtime/support/operations;
- capability/module uplift where validated.

Arvectum OS should normally be sold through customer outcomes rather than as an empty generic platform until independent platform demand is evidenced.

## 5. Work ↔ owned-capability routing

The Company should optimize for the customer's and Arvectum's joint economics, not protect one revenue line.

### Work → owned capability

When a Work stream becomes stable, large and recurring, compare:

`annual Work cost`

against

`owned-capability implementation + support + residual human cost`.

If owned capability is economically superior, surface the handoff rather than keeping the customer on Work solely for recurring revenue.

### Owned capability → Work

An AI Company customer may use Work for:
- burst capacity;
- temporary workloads;
- capabilities not worth owning;
- bounded external execution.

No automatic runtime coupling is implied.

## 6. Capability portfolio

Capabilities are admitted by evidence, not by repository existence.

### Procurement / Tender
Anchor: **Arvectum Tender Agent**.

Roles:
- standalone revenue/pilot product;
- procurement capability candidate for AI Company;
- candidate Work execution capability for bounded procurement-document work;
- reference domain for governed higher-consequence execution.

### Marketing / Creative
Anchor: **Creative Test Agent**.

Roles:
- standalone product where customer demand exists;
- marketing/creative capability candidate;
- candidate Work supply for bounded analysis/reporting.

### Data acquisition / normalization
Evidence: **Discount Parser**, **Doors Parser**, source-specific extraction/normalization work.

Potential reusable contract only where multiple real consumers require the same flow:

`collect → normalize → deduplicate → classify → preserve provenance → structured result`.

Do not create a universal parser or Data Platform without named consumers and economics.

### Future capabilities

Prefer capabilities discovered from:
1. repeated paid Work demand;
2. repeated AI Company implementation need;
3. recurring internal Arvectum workload with credible external reuse.

## 7. Arvectum OS

Arvectum OS remains the domain-neutral Executable Organizational Model / governed-execution substrate.

OS owns admitted universal semantics such as Identity, Organization, Authority, Records, Relationships, Provenance, Documents, Knowledge, Security, Persistence and Governed Execution.

Company/Product-specific tender, marketing, parser, Work-marketplace and pricing semantics remain outside OS unless independently generalized and admitted through OS governance.

AI is an executor, not the source of Organizational Authority.

## 8. Proxy Launcher

Arvectum Proxy Launcher is supporting infrastructure/ecosystem.

Default role:
- useful free/standalone utility;
- distribution and brand trust;
- Russian desktop/Linux credibility;
- sovereignty, packaging, release and signing proving ground.

Do not force monetization or hard core-product dependency without evidence.

## 9. Customer entry paths

Three valid entry paths remain:

1. **Outcome now:** painful digital work → Work pilot → repeat stream → remain Work or handoff to owned capability.
2. **Structural transformation:** operating bottleneck → AI Company discovery → Arvectum OS + validated capabilities.
3. **Narrow need:** Tender / Marketing / Parser / utility → standalone product → possible expansion later.

Do not force every customer into Arvectum OS or Work.

## 10. Company economics

### Work metrics
- paid accepted units/batches;
- repeat purchase;
- revenue/GMV where relevant;
- execution and verification cost;
- rework/exception cost;
- contribution margin;
- acquisition effort;
- Owner intervention;
- time-to-result.

### AI Company metrics
- paid discovery/implementation revenue;
- delivery cost/time;
- recurring runtime/support revenue;
- capability reuse;
- support burden;
- customer ROI/value evidence;
- margin;
- acquisition effort;
- Owner intervention.

### Capability metrics

A reusable capability must measurably:
- lower marginal delivery cost;
- increase quality/throughput;
- shorten deployment;
- or create independent revenue,

without disproportionate support burden.

## 11. Strategic moat hypotheses

Potential cumulative moat:
- Russian legal/payment/infrastructure compatibility;
- RUB/SBP/digital-ruble readiness where applicable;
- local/self-hosted execution and data sovereignty;
- organization-first authority/governance through Arvectum OS;
- real execution/acceptance history;
- reusable capabilities learned from paid work;
- Work↔owned economic routing;
- replaceable models/runtimes;
- Arvectum Company as real dogfooding reference organization.

These remain hypotheses until evidenced.

## 12. What Arvectum will not optimize for

Do not optimize for:
- maximum number of agents or repositories;
- six separate GTM motions;
- marketplace infrastructure before paid demand;
- generic platform engineering without admitted consumers;
- premature universal modules;
- keeping Work customers when owned capability is clearly better;
- forcing OS on every customer;
- unlimited AI authority;
- hidden human completion presented as autonomy.

## 13. Capital-allocation order

Default Company strategic order:

1. obligations / continuity / cash / material risk;
2. external revenue and customer acceptance;
3. Work demand + paid-stream proof and AI Company customer proof;
4. capability reuse with measurable economics;
5. required Arvectum OS platform work;
6. speculative infrastructure/new-product work.

Owner may explicitly override this order.

## 14. Company strategic success condition

Arvectum succeeds strategically when it can repeatedly:

1. identify a costly digital business function;
2. sell/deliver it as accepted machine work with positive economics;
3. decide when external Work versus owned capability is economically preferable;
4. deploy owned capability on Arvectum OS where justified;
5. reuse validated capabilities across customers without violating authority/data boundaries;
6. operate the system without the Owner becoming the routine execution bottleneck.
